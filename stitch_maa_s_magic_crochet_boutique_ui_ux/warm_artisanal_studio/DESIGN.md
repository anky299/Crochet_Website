## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the intimate, tactile, and heartfelt essence of a generational mother-and-daughter crochet studio based in Lohardaga, Jharkhand. Rooted in artisanal craftsmanship, slow living, and heartfelt warmth, the aesthetic steers clear of mass-market e-commerce sterility. Instead, it invites the user into a sunlit room filled with skeins of combed cotton, soft linen surfaces, and botanical greens.

The design movement merges **Artisanal Editorial** with **Tactile Warmth**. Generous breathing room, deliberate typography pairings, organic curved contours, and paper-like depth evoke an authentic handcrafted boutique. The interface feels personal, grounding, and gentle—instilling immediate trust and deep emotional resonance with collectors of slow-crafted textile art.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from raw, dyed organic fibers, sun-dried clay, and soothing leaves:

- **Primary (`#C87573` - Dusty Rose):** Used for focal action items, artisan badges, curated tags, and highlights. It communicates tenderness and devotion.
- **Secondary (`#6C8E65` - Muted Sage):** Anchors botanical harmony, in-stock indicators, organic material certifications, and direct WhatsApp communication channels.
- **Tertiary (`#D4A373` - Spun Yarn Gold):** Reserved for subtle craft accents, limited-edition ribbons, and fine decorative hairline dividers.
- **Neutral Deep (`#36221D` - Cozy Chocolate):** Replaces harsh pure black for all primary titles, body text, and structural contours, preserving a rich, comfortable reading temperature.
- **Surface & Canvas:**
  - Base Canvas (`#FFF9F3`): Warm cream/ivory foundation that softens eye strain.
  - Surface Linen (`#FAF0E6`): Card containers, product tiles, and floating sheets.
  - Elevated Surface (`#F4E4D4`): Interactive panels, pressed states, and dialogs.
  - Subtle Tint / Delicate Blush (`#F5DED8`): Secondary badges, hover fills, and gentle focus rings.
  - Text Muted (`#7D6A65`): Warm taupe for secondary metadata, yarn specifications, and timestamp captions.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy harmonizes the literary heritage of **Playfair Display** with the modern, humanistic legibility of **Plus Jakarta Sans**. 

- **Headlines & Editorial Callouts:** Playfair Display introduces an artisanal, literary cadence reminiscent of heirloom craft catalogs. It is typeset with generous line heights to prevent tight vertical tension.
- **Accents & Story Vignettes:** For short studio flourishes, signature tags, and artisan quotes, delicate italic serif styling or specialized hand-lettered cursive accents are applied selectively to secondary phrasing (e.g., *"Hand-knitted with love in Jharkhand"*), never for critical metadata or functional labels.
- **Body & Functional UI:** Plus Jakarta Sans is applied across all long-form product stories, dimensions, care instructions, and UI elements. Its open apertures and balanced proportions maintain effortless legibility across hand-held mobile devices.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy uses a flexible, content-conscious fluid grid anchored by serene, intentional whitespace. Studio compositions must never feel compressed or dense; breathing room conveys the patience of hand-crocheting.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column layout with 2rem gutters and 3.5rem outer canvas margins. Showcases asymmetric editorial compositions, pairing large-scale woven photography with story modules.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout with 1.25rem gutters and 2rem outer margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with 0.75rem gutters and 1rem safe outer margins. Touch targets adhere to minimum 48px zones, and product carousels feature graceful edge-bleed with soft snapping.
- **Vertical Spacing Rhythm:** Section breaks deploy `space-xl` for calm transitions. Cards and nested content blocks maintain comfortable internal padding using `space-md` to `space-lg`.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through **ambient warm shadows** and **tonal layering**, strictly avoiding cold grey drop shadows:

- **Tonal Stepping:** Surfaces stack naturally without harsh contrast. The background (`#FFF9F3`) hosts card containers in soft linen beige (`#FAF0E6`), which in turn hold elevated interaction modules in `#F4E4D4`.
- **Sunlit Ambient Shadows:** Shadows carry an organic, amber-chocolate tint (`rgba(54, 34, 29, 0.06)`) diffused with broad blur radii (16px–28px) and minimal y-axis displacement. This simulates the natural diffused light of an afternoon studio window.
- **Ghost Outlines:** Where visual separation is required over photography or rich surfaces, use ultra-soft borders: `1px solid rgba(125, 106, 101, 0.15)`. Avoid black or high-contrast structural dividers.

## Shapes

The shape system honors the continuous curves and loops of yarn and crochet stitches. Sharp, pointed vertices are eliminated from the user interface.

- **Standard Containers & Cards:** Base roundedness of `1rem` (`rounded-lg`) to `1.5rem` (`rounded-xl`), creating inviting, touch-friendly modules.
- **Buttons, Tags & Interactive Badges:** Fully rounded pill silhouettes (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) provide an organic contrast against larger rectangular image cards.
- **Media Framing:** Image containers for crochet pieces feature generous rounded corners (`1.25rem` to `1.75rem`) to reinforce the gentle, cushion-like quality of handmade textiles.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Studio Action (Dusty Rose):** Fills in `#C87573` with warm cream `#FFF9F3` text. Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded with `0.875rem` vertical and `1.75rem` horizontal spacing. Subtle lift on hover with an ambient warm shadow.
- **WhatsApp Direct Connect (Sage Green):** Styled in `#6C8E65` with `#FFF9F3` text. Used for custom crochet commissions, yarn consultations, and real-time updates. Accompanied by a clean botanical or chat icon.
- **Secondary / Ghost Button:** Transparent fill with a `1.5px` border in `#36221D` or `#C87573`, using deep chocolate text for understated actions.

### Cards (Product & Story)
- Built on `#FAF0E6` linen surfaces with a gentle `1.25rem` corner radius.
- Includes a 1:1 or 4:5 image frame with soft inner edge blending, title in Playfair Display (`headline-sm`), price/availability in Plus Jakarta Sans (`label-lg`), and an artisanal pill tag (e.g., *"100% Organic Cotton"*, *"Made by Maa"*).
- Interactive card states employ a soft upward translation (`-3px`) and expanded ambient shadow.

### Chips & Badges
- Miniature pill containers for material specs, crochet stitch types, and availability.
- Tinted background in `#F5DED8` with `#C87573` text, or `#FAF0E6` with a delicate `#D4A373` border for limited editions.

### Input Fields & Inquiry Forms
- Rounded corners (`0.875rem`), background filled with `#FAF0E6`, and borders in `1px solid rgba(125, 106, 101, 0.2)`.
- Active focus transitions border to `#C87573` with a soft outer ring in `#F5DED8`.
- Placeholder text in `#7D6A65`; user input rendered in `#36221D`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Custom circular and rounded-square forms using `#FAF0E6` fill.
- Selected state fills with `#6C8E65` (sage) or `#C87573` (rose), displaying a warm ivory checkmark or dot.

### Specialized Studio Components
- **Yarn & Stitch Swatch Selector:** Circular interactive swatches showcasing real macro textures of dyed threads, bordered with a delicate brass ring when selected.
- **Artisanal Story Ribbon:** A full-width linen strip (`#F4E4D4`) highlighted with subtle yarn-gold accent lines (`#D4A373`) celebrating the slow handmade craft timeline (e.g., *"18 Hours of Hand-Weaving"*).
